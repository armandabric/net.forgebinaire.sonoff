Sonoff Hydro brings your Sonoff Hydro One Zigbee smart water valve into Homey. Turn the water on or off, get an alert the moment a leak or a water shortage is detected, and start a watering run for a set duration or a set amount of water straight from a Flow.

The valve talks Zigbee directly to Homey, so there is no separate cloud account or companion app to manage. Combine it with Homey's own Flow scheduling to build exactly the watering routine your garden needs, and let Homey warn you the instant something needs attention.
